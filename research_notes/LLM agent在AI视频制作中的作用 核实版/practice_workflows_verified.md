**Dear Upstairs Neighbors**
- **Video-to-video, dailies, paintovers. CONFIRMED on No Film School** (Jourdan Aldredge interviewing director Connie He, 2026-06-19): "Our film took a slightly different approach than a typical text-to-video or image-to-video workflow. Crucially, we didn't rely on text prompting. Having tight control over animation performance, timing, and rhythm was extremely important to the story, so we decided to explore a video-to-video workflow." "Our R&D team built a bespoke pipeline… We implemented controlnets and mask editing tools for both video and image models". "We reviewed shots in dailies every day… Sometimes the solution came from an artist doing a paintover, other times we needed to develop new tools… it became a very organic, iterative process." "For us, the goal was never to save time". — [No Film School](https://nofilmschool.com/dear-upstairs-neighbors-hybrid-ai-short-film)
- **Custom fine-tuned models: NOT FOUND in the NFS interview body; CONFIRMED on the Google blog** (Cassidy Curtis, Supervising Animator, and Sarah Rumbley, VFX Supervisor, 2026-01-26): "our researchers built tools that allowed our artists to fine-tune custom Veo and Imagen models on their artwork, teaching the models new visual concepts from just a few example images." "Using text-to-video with the fine-tuned Veo model produced scenes that looked like Ada, but their movement was random, uncontrolled, and often bizarre. Text alone can't convey the nuance and specificity needed for narrative animated filmmaking." "Even with the control provided by fine-tuning and video-to-video workflows, none of our final shots were created in a single 'one-click' generation. Just as in any film production, we critiqued each shot in our 'dailies' reviews, going through several rounds of feedback". "we used Veo's upscaling capability to bring our final shots to 4K resolution". — [Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/dear-upstairs-neighbors/)

**Dave Clark at No Film School** ("How Dave Clark of Promise Studios is Striving to Bring Balance to the World of AI Filmmaking", Jourdan Aldredge, 2025-11-04; a roundtable at Adobe MAX, which hosted the outlet)
- It is not a step-by-step workflow. What it contains: his film "My Friend Zeph" is a hybrid, "live action sequences with actors on set and on a blue screen", with backgrounds swapped and several models mixed inside Adobe Firefly ("some models do some things better than others"); Nano Banana was used to de-age the lead from childhood photos with her permission, and Veo to animate her and the robot. — [No Film School](https://nofilmschool.com/dave-clark-interview-adobe-max)
- On finishing for the big screen: "we were able to show how you get something that's AI-generated to the same or at least close to the same type of color space as the live action scenes… How do you go from eight 8-bit to 16-bit color space with this type of stuff? That's important if you want something to be on a big screen or to pass QC at a streamer like Netflix or Amazon." — [No Film School](https://nofilmschool.com/dave-clark-interview-adobe-max)
- On difficulty: "Using AI at the professional level is very hard. It's not easy… you have to really bring filmmaking expertise into the room because audiences aren't going to settle for less… It's actually a lot harder, especially with long form, because of character consistency issues. With generative AI, you often get generative drift." — [No Film School](https://nofilmschool.com/dave-clark-interview-adobe-max)

An animated feature in pre-production uses "custom workflows and Python code" to tie AI tools to 3D products. — [No Film School](https://nofilmschool.com/dave-clark-interview-adobe-max)

**Curious Refuge, "How to Create a Professional AI Film | Pro Workflow"** (Tyler Smith, 2026-04-14; an educator owned by Promise; the written post is a short companion to a video)

- On planning: "Even basic sketches help you maintain continuity and avoid that 'random AI look.'" On images: "Generate multiple variations every single time. In this game, iteration is everything." On the edit: "This is where the film actually comes to life through cutting, sound design, and pacing." **MiniMax Hailuo knowledge base** (three pages, all dated 2026-07-27, signed "Hailuo AI Studio — We build the Hailuo AI creative suite"; each calls itself "a scenario model based on internal studio heuristics, not a controlled lab study"; the pages cite NIST, the EU AI Act and McKinsey for claims those documents do not make, so treat the authority as vendor marketing)
- *Trajectory matching.* The problem named: "the jarring 'jump' that occurs when two clips fail to match in motion trajectory. If Shot A is a slow, sweeping pan to the right and Shot B starts with a static frame, the narrative momentum collapses." Clip length assumed: 4-6 seconds. — [Hailuo, trajectory](https://hailuoai.video/knowledge/trajectory-matching-seamless-ai-video-cuts)
- No numeric camera commands: "Specifying 'Pan 30 degrees left' or 'Zoom 15%' typically results in inconsistent outputs. The AI does not interpret geometric angles". Use comparative wording relative to a "Master Motion Reference", e.g. "Continue the slow, deliberate dolly-in from the previous shot, maintaining the same forward momentum." — [Hailuo, trajectory](https://hailuoai.video/knowledge/trajectory-matching-seamless-ai-video-cuts)
- "2-Second Rule" for a cut on action: "the primary motion in both clips should cover a similar visual distance in roughly two seconds… If the subject moves across 20% of the frame in the final two seconds of Clip A, they should ideally move across a similar 20% in the first two seconds of Clip B." Judged by eye; "not a hard-coded software setting". — [Hailuo, trajectory](https://hailuoai.video/knowledge/trajectory-matching-seamless-ai-video-cuts)
- Anchoring: "Generate your master composition using a high-end Text-to-Image (T2I) tool. Use this exact image as the input for every shot in the sequence. Save the 'Seed'… While seeds in AI video are not a guarantee of identical motion, they significantly 'tighten' the probability envelope". Selection rule: "We look for the shot that best matches the exit trajectory of the previous clip." Keep motion "gross" rather than "fine": "large-scale movements like walking or turning, which the AI can track more reliably than complex hand-object manipulations." Post: speed-ramp the head or tail of a clip to stitch trajectories, upscale, and "A unified LUT… applied in post will lock the shots together visually." "Hailuo AI is a generation engine, not an editor." — [Hailuo, trajectory](https://hailuoai.video/knowledge/trajectory-matching-seamless-ai-video-cuts)
- *Shot/reverse-shot spatial logic.* Why it drifts: "without a spatial anchor, the model has no 'memory' of where that character sits in relation to the window, the door, or the person they are talking to." Remedy: state gaze per shot ("Shot A: 'Character looking slightly camera-right.' Shot B: 'Character looking slightly camera-left.'"); "Avoid vague terms like 'looking at the other person.'" Example: "Character gaze directed at off-screen position, 15 degrees camera-left." (This uses a number, against the trajectory page's own advice.) — [Hailuo, shot/reverse-shot](https://hailuoai.video/knowledge/director-guide-shot-reverse-shot-spatial-logic)
- Master shot first: "The most common mistake we observe in professional AI workflows is generating close-ups before establishing the environment." Generate "the widest possible establishing shot… that includes all characters and key environmental landmarks" and treat it as "your spatial source of truth". — [Hailuo, shot/reverse-shot](https://hailuoai.video/knowledge/director-guide-shot-reverse-shot-spatial-logic)
- Coverage and retakes: "generate 3-5 variations of each angle"; "Expect to 'reroll' (regenerate) each shot at least twice". Lens: specify e.g. "85mm focal length" for shallow depth of field, which "helps mask minor environmental inconsistencies in the background". Motion: prompt "subtle nods, blinks, or slight leans. Avoid prompting for complex physics like 'drinking coffee' or 'shaking hands'". Lighting: "Describe the lighting in every prompt (e.g., 'Key light from camera-left, 3200k color temperature')" so shadows do not flip on the reverse. Use "Fixed camera" or "Slow, steady pan" to stop unwanted zooms. The cut is made in an NLE, trimming "to the exact frame where the eyelines align." — [Hailuo, shot/reverse-shot](https://hailuoai.video/knowledge/director-guide-shot-reverse-shot-spatial-logic)
- *Multi-axis camera control.* "2+1 Rule": "Define no more than two primary spatial movements and one secondary quality per generation." Beyond that "the likelihood of 'hallucinated' motion or subject distortion increases by an estimated factor of three, based on our internal workflow modeling" (no data shown). The "Stitch" workflow: generate a 4-second "Hero" clip from an I2V reference, log seed and reference, "extract the final frame", and start the next axis of movement from it: "By using the last frame of Shot A as the first frame of Shot B, you ensure that the lighting, textures, and subject positioning remain identical." "You may need 2 or 3 'rerolls' to get the perfect motion". — [Hailuo, multi-axis](https://hailuoai.video/pages/knowledge/ai-video-multi-axis-camera-control)

**The Eian workflow as packaged in three open-source skills** (all anecdotal, single-creator practice; repositories cloned and read: xdlkc/ai-video-production, last commit 2026-11-06; AsLSX99/ai-video-workflow, 2026-11-07; kowloonzh/ai-video-workflow, 2026-11-06)
- *Stated aim and cost rule.* "用尽量少的钱、尽量低的抽卡率，做出一条自己满意的 AI 视频。" "提示词改几个字不要钱，重画一张图基本不要钱，但重新生成视频按秒付费。" The ladder: "文字里能发现的错 → 不留到图上；图上能发现的错 → 不留到视频上；视频里能修的错 → 不重拍（先剪掉 / 先写代码修）；必须重拍的镜头 → 先改提示词或首尾帧，再重拍，只拍这一镜". — [xdlkc SKILL.md](https://github.com/xdlkc/ai-video-production/blob/main/SKILL.md)
- *Roles.* User: "选方向、确认画风、批准花钱；看动态、听声音". Director (Claude Code or another main agent): "写故事和分镜、写所有提示词、调用其他模型、抽帧放大检查、用代码做后期". Painter (Codex): 设定图、样帧、每镜首帧和尾帧. Animator (Seedance): first and last frame into a few seconds of video. Tools: ffmpeg and Python. AsLSX99's variant makes Codex the director and says the original allows it ("如果你就是 Codex，就用你自己的生图工具"). — [xdlkc README](https://github.com/xdlkc/ai-video-production/blob/main/README.md); [AsLSX99/ai-video-workflow](https://github.com/AsLSX99/ai-video-workflow)
- *Before starting.* Ask four values: 片长、画幅、总预算、要不要配乐和字幕. Check Codex, ffmpeg, Python and `ARK_API_KEY`. — [xdlkc SKILL.md](https://github.com/xdlkc/ai-video-production/blob/main/SKILL.md)
- *Step 1, story.* Six tests: fits real-world logic ("为什么会这样？东西从哪来？够得着吗？"); can be said in one sentence ("说不出来就说明剧情平淡，回去改"); the most arresting image comes first, with the second it appears; bystanders react like people, each turn written as "问题 → 主角怎么解决 → 旁人什么反应", with reactions staggered; the model can shoot it, avoiding "人物交叉、手拿小东西、文字、镜子与倒影、左右不对称特征、一镜内换光、一人连做三个动作、俯拍看不到脸、配角太多"; and "结构首选片段式：镜头之间不用接戏，最稳；连贯式最容易穿帮". Then a fresh agent reads only the story and only finds faults. — [xdlkc SKILL.md](https://github.com/xdlkc/ai-video-production/blob/main/SKILL.md)
- *Step 2, shot list.* "这一步决定成片质量，重要性甚至高于剧情。" Each shot states 景别、机位、运镜、谁往哪走（写画面左右）、靠什么表情推进、"是什么在动"、怎么转到下一镜. Rules: "全片平视正面最多两次"; something moves in every frame; no run of same-size shots; camera movement needs a reason; head or eyes at about 38% or 62% of frame width. Generation length per shot: "片中时长向上取整，尽量多留 0.5 秒". With a reference film, the director measures it frame by frame first. — [xdlkc SKILL.md](https://github.com/xdlkc/ai-video-production/blob/main/SKILL.md); [xdlkc prompts.md](https://github.com/xdlkc/ai-video-production/blob/main/references/prompts.md)
- *Step 3, look and palette.* A colour table per scene is fixed before the first image; the director draws a 1920×1080 colour-block sketch per shot in code, assembles a contact sheet for the user, and converts it to black and white: "人和背景分不开就说明明暗对比不够，重配". Character and prop sheets fix asymmetric features in words ("纹身在左小臂"). — [xdlkc SKILL.md](https://github.com/xdlkc/ai-video-production/blob/main/SKILL.md)
- *Step 4, first and last frames*, called "降低抽卡率最关键的一步". The director writes what each frame shows and a reviewer passes it before anything is drawn; the fuller frame (usually the last) is drawn first and the other derived from it; same-location shots are edited from the previous image; a push-in's last frame can be a crop of the first; both frames must be the same size. Eight-point check per image, including "动作要用到的东西，首帧里必须有", bystanders placed in advance facing camera, different poses for multiple people ("不然模型会让所有人同时动"), space left for motion, one wind direction, a phone-size view, and overlaying first and last frame for misregistration ("墙的颜色不一样，视频里墙就会慢慢变色"). — [xdlkc SKILL.md](https://github.com/xdlkc/ai-video-production/blob/main/SKILL.md); [xdlkc prompts.md](https://github.com/xdlkc/ai-video-production/blob/main/references/prompts.md)
- *Step 5, video prompt.* "出问题先怀疑提示词，再怀疑模型。" Seven fixed sections: 【画风】【镜头】【构图与配色】【人物】【逐秒】【声音】【约束】, opening "0–0.2 秒：画面完全静止，和首帧一模一样" and closing "最后 0.3 秒：所有人完全停住，画面与尾帧完全一致". Pitfall table: one or two main actions per shot; directions in screen terms; props must be visible in the first frame; quantities written out; "写「不要怎么样」经常无效…尽量改正面表述"; "提示词里的时间只算大概，多留时间，后期用剪辑和变速对节拍". — [xdlkc pitfalls.md](https://github.com/xdlkc/ai-video-production/blob/main/references/pitfalls.md)
- *Gate before spending.* "提交前必须另开审核 agent，材料只给：提示词、首帧、尾帧、设定图、分镜表、配色规则；输出只有「通过 / 不通过 + 逐条要改什么」。这一步是低抽卡率的关键。" The reviewer reads only and changes nothing; after a pass the director quotes a price and the user approves. Why a separate agent: "新 agent 像第一次看片的观众，能暴露主 agent 因长时间对话产生的自动脑补". What it has caught, per the two other repositories citing the article: "两张图里的杯子大小差了 13%、帽檐一张有一张没有、提示词里左右写反了". — [xdlkc SKILL.md](https://github.com/xdlkc/ai-video-production/blob/main/SKILL.md); [kowloonzh/ai-video-workflow](https://github.com/kowloonzh/ai-video-workflow); [AsLSX99/ai-video-workflow](https://github.com/AsLSX99/ai-video-workflow)
- *Step 6, generation.* 720p, upscaled later; "不拍测试片，直接拍第一个正式镜头，能用就赚了"; "新做法先用一两个最难的镜头试，可行再批量提交"; the cheaper model for simple shots. On a fault: "剪掉 → 用代码修 → 重拍（先改提示词或首尾帧，只拍这一镜）", old clips kept, "最后一起挑" ("新片段不一定比旧的好"). — [xdlkc SKILL.md](https://github.com/xdlkc/ai-video-production/blob/main/SKILL.md)
- *Step 7, post, all in code.* Designed transitions; grade by story section, "只调彩度与亮度，不动色相…不加颗粒/柔光/暗角" (the opposite of the grain advice elsewhere; the example look is flat 2D animation); decode Seedance mp4 as BT.601, deliver as BT.709; lanczos 720p→1080p with light sharpening, no interpolation; beats measured on the original track only; check 3 frames each side of every cut. — [xdlkc SKILL.md](https://github.com/xdlkc/ai-video-production/blob/main/SKILL.md)
- *Step 8, final review.* A new agent gets "只给成片、不给任何结论", decodes all of it, takes several full-resolution frames per shot and 3 each side of each cut, writes the story it understood before listing what it did not, checks each shot for repeated actions, prop counts, jumps, colour jumps, face deformation and text, and "明确写出「查不了的东西」（动态手感、声音）". — [xdlkc SKILL.md](https://github.com/xdlkc/ai-video-production/blob/main/SKILL.md)

### Inferences
- The Hailuo guides and the Eian skills agree, from the vendor side and the creator side, on four things: anchor every shot to an image; ask for gross motion and one or two moves; write direction in screen terms; expect two or three draws on a hard match. They differ on chaining: Hailuo chains last frame to first frame inside one extended move, while the Eian skills prefer a "片段式" structure in which shots do not have to join at all.
- The Eian skills are the only source in this thread that say where the LLM is blind and build the process around it: the reviewer checks stills and extracted frames, and motion and sound are named as unchecked in every report.
- Independent review appears in the skills as a cost device (it sits before the paid step), not as a quality ritual. Its claimed catches are still-image and text mismatches (a 13% size difference, a missing hat brim, left/right swapped), which is what a frame-reading agent can see.
- "Render the hardest shot first" in the earlier notes is slightly off: the skill says no test footage, shoot a real shot first, and when a method is new, try it on one or two of the hardest shots before the batch.

### Takeaway
The confirmed figures fall into three kinds that should not be mixed: whole-production ratios that include exploration (64 clips per final shot; "300:1" by duration), per-shot draw counts in production lines with assets locked (2-3 to 5, up to "十几次甚至几十次" for demanding shots), and worst-case image counts (200-1,000). Only the Hell Grind figure is an actual count; the rest are estimates from memory or round numbers in interviews.

### Inferences
- Air Head's "300:1" does not agree with its own count. 300 times 90 seconds is 7.5 hours of footage, which at 10-20 seconds each would be about 1,350 to 2,700 generations, not "hundreds". If "hundreds" is right (say 300 to 900 clips averaging 15 s), the duration ratio is roughly 50:1 to 150:1. Cederberg flags the estimate himself ("My math is bad"). The report should cite it as "hundreds of generations for a minute and a half, by his estimate about 300:1".
- Hell Grind by duration: 16,181 clips × about 15 s ≈ 67 hours generated for 25 minutes, about 160:1 by duration, against 64:1 by clip count. So on a like-for-like duration basis the 2026 feature and the 2024 short are of the same order, and the clip-count ratio looks smaller only because each final shot (about 5.9 s on average) uses part of a 15-second generation. Both steps are my arithmetic on reported figures.
- Comparable basis for planning: with assets and first frames locked, production lines report 2-5 draws per accepted shot; demanding shots run to "十几次甚至几十次"; whole-project totals that include exploration and look development run one to two orders of magnitude higher. The two kinds of number answer different questions (cost of a planned shot against cost of finding the film).
- The one before-and-after claim (十几次 → 两三次) credits a workflow that front-loads assets and script review, and the same speaker says the checking and repair work grew. It is testimony from one company, not a measurement.

### Gaps
- No source separates exploration from production draws inside one project, so no true "draws per planned shot" exists for any praised film.
- No ratio was found for an H3 (MiniMax) production. The Hailuo figures are vendor heuristics.
- The invideo "164 clips, 41 used" figure in the earlier notes was not re-read.
- No source reports how often a take that passed a still-frame check was later rejected on motion or sound.
